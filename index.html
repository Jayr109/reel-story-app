The Reel Story uploader

A private publishing tool for the YouTube channel The Reel Story.

This application is used only by the owner of the YouTube channel The Reel Story to upload that channel's own videos, set their thumbnails and captions, schedule them, and read the channel's own performance statistics.

It is not offered to the public. No one other than the channel owner signs in to it, and it does not access anyone else's account or data.
