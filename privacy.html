Privacy policy

The Reel Story uploader. Last updated 26 November 2026.

Who uses this application

This application is a private tool operated by, and used only by, the owner of the YouTube channel The Reel Story. It is not offered to other users.

What it accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, the application is granted access to that one YouTube channel through Google's YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API. It uses that access only to upload the channel's own videos, set their thumbnails and captions, schedule their publication, and read the channel's own view, retention and engagement statistics. It also reads public YouTube data, such as trending videos and public view counts, to research topics.

How data is stored

The sign-in token and the channel's statistics are stored only on the channel owner's own computer. They are not sold, shared, or sent to any third party, and they are not used for advertising.

Other people's data

The application does not collect, store or process personal information about viewers or any other person.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account permissions.

The application's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
